Eunomia Labs, Inc.

Eunomia Labs, Inc. builds and maintains the Eunomia open-source projects for eBPF runtimes, GPU observability, and AI agent infrastructure.

The company operates eunomia.dev and develops the projects in the eunomia-bpf GitHub organization. Our work centers on eBPF runtime infrastructure: extending applications safely, observing and extending GPU workloads, and giving AI agents a system layer for observability and policy enforcement.

The company is based in San Francisco, California.

What we build

ProjectWhat it does
bpftimeA userspace eBPF runtime for uprobes, syscalls, and application extensions, compatible with kernel eBPF toolchains. Its GPU support runs eBPF programs inside CUDA and ROCm kernels on NVIDIA and AMD GPUs.
gpu_extExtensible OS policies for GPU scheduling and memory management, written as eBPF programs.
AgentSightSystem-level observability for AI agents using eBPF, with zero instrumentation of the agent itself.
ActPlaneOS-level safety, security, and governance controls for AI agents, with policy-as-code for process trees, files, network effects, and workflow gates.
eunomia-bpf and wasm-bpfToolchains for building, packaging, and distributing eBPF programs, including as WebAssembly modules.
TutorialsThe bpf-developer-tutorial series, plus CUDA, CUPTI, and NVBit tutorials for GPU programming, profiling, and instrumentation.

Research

Our projects grow out of systems research. bpftime appeared at OSDI 2025 as "Extending Applications Safely and Efficiently", AgentSight at PACMI 2025, and SchedCP at MLforSystem 2025. The papers page lists the full set with PDFs, artifacts, and explainers.

Open source

All code is developed in the open at github.com/eunomia-bpf, and each repository states its license. Issues and GitHub Discussions are the main collaboration channels, and the activity reports record the organization's public activity every month.

Working with us

Eunomia Labs supports teams that adopt these projects in production, from evaluation to integration. Products describes the product lines and enterprise support. To reach us directly, email yusheng@eunomia.dev.
