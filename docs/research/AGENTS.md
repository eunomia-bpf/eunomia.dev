# Daily Report publication rules

These rules apply to every public page under `docs/research/`.

- Keep existing `/research/` and `/zh/research/` routes stable, but do not explain route compatibility or naming history in reader-facing copy.
- The section index should introduce the reports and list current entries. Do not publish an editorial contract, process checklist, or internal content policy on the page.
- Do not place an automation or review-status banner at the beginning of a page.
- Use only the shared, low-contrast, single-line footer note rendered by the application. Do not duplicate that note in Markdown.
- Avoid generic public headings such as `Scope`, `Limitations`, `Scope and limitations`, `适用范围`, or `局限性`. Explain where an argument may fail under a concrete reader-facing heading such as `What would change this conclusion?` or `哪些结果会改变这个判断？`.
- Each report should still identify specific gaps in existing research or production practice and develop a small number of technically testable directions with both academic and deployment value.
- Keep facts, interpretation, proposed mechanisms, and open questions distinguishable in the prose.
